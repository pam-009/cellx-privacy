CellX Privacy Policy

CellX: Battery Life Extender is built with a strict zero-server, offline-first architecture. Your battery telemetry and usage habits never leave your device.

Effective Date: November 2026  •  Package: com.gangopadhyay.cellx
Zero Cloud Servers No backend database, no account login, and no automatic cloud sync.
Zero Tracking or Ads No third-party ad SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no data brokers.
User-Controlled Only Export or permanently delete all local history anytime in one tap.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CellX does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, or battery logs with the developers or any third party.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All charging sessions, temperature logs, battery health estimates, and 7-day power learning patterns are stored exclusively inside a private local database on your Android device. Uninstalling CellX or tapping Clear Data inside Settings permanently erases all stored records.

3. Optional Network & Smart Plug Usage

CellX operates 100% offline by default. Network communication occurs only if you explicitly configure an optional smart plug integration (such as local LAN webhooks, Tuya Local, Kasa Local, Shelly, Tasmota, MQTT, or Govee API) to automatically cut off AC power at your chosen charge limit. Any API keys or local IP addresses you enter are stored strictly on your device and are automatically redacted from diagnostic exports.

4. Device Permissions Explained

  • Notifications & Foreground Service: Used solely to deliver real-time charge limit alarms, thermal safety alerts, and reliable background battery monitoring.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption (Optional): Prevents Android from pausing charge-cutoff alarms while your screen is off.
  • Overlay Permission (Optional): Used only if you enable the draggable Floating Battery HUD.
  • Usage Access (Optional): Used locally on-device to identify high-drain apps in Device Intelligence; never uploaded.

5. Voluntary Diagnostic Reports & Feedback

If you choose to tap Send Feedback / Report Bug, CellX generates a sanitized plain-text diagnostic snapshot for your review before opening your email client. Sensitive credentials (API keys, local IPs, MAC addresses, and webhook URLs) are always stripped automatically.

6. Children's Privacy

Because CellX collects zero personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
